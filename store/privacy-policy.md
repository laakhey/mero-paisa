# Mero Paisa — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Mero Paisa ("the app") is a personal expense tracker published by Laakhey ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is kept, and how you can delete it.

## The short version

- Everything you enter is stored **on your device**. You can use the app fully without an account.
- If you **choose** to turn on cloud sync, your data is also stored on our server so your devices stay in step.
- We do not show ads, sell data, or use analytics or tracking.
- You can delete your cloud data or your whole account from inside the app at any time.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the accounts, categories, labels, transactions, budgets and settings you enter in a database on your device. This data does not leave your device unless you turn on cloud sync, or you export it yourself (CSV export or backup file), in which case you choose where the file goes.

If you turn on **App lock**, your device's own fingerprint, face or passcode check is used. The app never receives or stores your biometric data.

## Cloud sync (optional)

Cloud sync is off until you sign in with Google from **Settings → Cloud sync**. When you sign in:

- **From your Google account** we receive your email address and basic profile information (name and profile picture) to identify your account. We do not get access to your Google password, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
- **Your app data** — accounts, categories, labels, transactions, budgets, split-bill groups (including the names you give the people in them and the bills you record) and your chosen currency — is copied to our database so your devices can sync.

Your data is stored with **Supabase** (our database and sign-in provider) in [REGION]. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only access its own data. Data is not end-to-end encrypted, so the service operators and our administrators are technically able to access it; we only do so when needed to operate the service or when legally required.

We use these service providers only to run the app: **Google** (sign-in) and **Supabase** (database and sign-in). We do not share your data with anyone else.

## Deleting your data and account

- **Cloud data only:** in the app, open **Settings → Cloud sync → Delete cloud data**. Everything stored on our server for your account is deleted; your account and the data on your device remain.
- **Your account:** in the app, open **Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account**. Your account and all of its cloud data are permanently deleted.
- **Without the app:** email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and all of its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.
- **Data on your device** is removed by deleting the app, or with **Settings → Erase all transactions**.

Deleted cloud data cannot be recovered.

## Data retention

Cloud data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. We do not keep copies after deletion, apart from routine server backups kept by Supabase for a limited period, after which they are overwritten.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
